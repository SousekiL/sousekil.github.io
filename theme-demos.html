02 / Type pairings

Three voices for an analytic introduction.

IBM Plex and Geist are self-hosted in this demo, so their actual letterforms can be compared without a runtime font service.

Current / editorial

Seeing society through data.

Best if the archive remains primarily literary and visual. Keep the old-style display, but accept that system sans rendering differs across devices.

IBM Plex / recommended

Risk, evidence, and method.

IBM Plex Sans for the self-introduction and IBM Plex Mono for exams, methods, dates, and tags. The most balanced research-and-technology option.

Geist / most technical

Data, models, and systems.

Geist makes the introduction more software-native; IBM Plex Mono preserves a precise information layer. Strongest contrast with the current editorial voice.
